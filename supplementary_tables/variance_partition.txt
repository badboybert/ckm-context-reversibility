HEADLINE (honest)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  same_tissue (unique R2=0.127) and same_intervention_family (unique R2=0.155) carry
  COMPARABLE, mutually-independent variance; same_platform carries essentially NONE
  (unique R2=0.005, p=0.28). Tissue and intervention BOTH survive panel permutation
  (p=0.00015, 5e-05); platform does not (p=0.272).
  => Reversibility is structured by shared CONTEXT (tissue AND intervention family
     co-govern) and is NOT an assay/batch artifact. This is the defensible form of the
     tissue-governor claim: within-tissue reversibility (+0.162) far exceeds cross-tissue
     (+0.020), the effect is permutation-robust and NOT platform-driven -- but the data do
     NOT support "tissue >> intervention": intervention family is an equal co-governor.

DISCLOSED LIMITATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Dyadic non-independence: each of 18 panels appears in ~17 of 153 pairs. Addressed via
     two-way cluster-robust SEs (both endpoints) + symmetric panel-level permutation +
     leave-one-panel-out jackknife. Naive OLS p-values are anti-conservative.
  2. Non-factorial design: at the PAIR level the three same_X indicators are near-orthogonal
     (|phi|<=0.13) so unique R2 is stable (jackknife ranges above), BUT the diet-family lift
     is confounded with adipose tissue (within-adipose only). Effects are not cleanly causal.
  3. same_layer OMITTED: all 18 panels are transcriptome -> constant, not partitionable.
  4. timepoint OMITTED: manifest has no structured post-intervention timepoint for these
     transcriptome panels (only sparse durability notes on proteome panels not in this set).
  5. intervention_family = coarse 4-bucket mechanism grouping with judgment calls
     (diet+exercise->diet; metformin+resistance->pharmacological; any bariatric->surgical).
